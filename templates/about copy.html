About Gigs_Anywhere

Gigs-anywhere is Kenya’s trusted online platform connecting clients with skilled workers across categories like Education, Home Services, Transport, Events, Tech, and Health & Wellness. Whether you need a private tutor in Eldoret, a plumber in Kakamega, or a boda rider for deliveries, we make it simple to find, book, and pay safely.

Our Mission

We empower local hustlers, freelancers, and SMEs by giving them visibility and credibility online. Our mission is to bridge the gap between clients and workers with verified profiles, instant notifications, and transparent ratings.

Why Choose Us

  • ✔ Verified worker profiles with ID and phone checks
  • ✔ Instant booking notifications via SMS and email
  • ✔ Clear payment rates and secure transactions
  • ✔ Ratings and reviews to build trust
  • ✔ Categories tailored to everyday needs in Kenya
